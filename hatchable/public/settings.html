Accounts & sign-in

How sign-in works today

This project is in personal visibility, which means every visitor signs in with their Hatchable account. Each authenticated visitor automatically gets their own private collection.

Google sign-in isn't currently a supported provider in Hatchable. The platform supports passwordless email (6-digit code) and passkeys (Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello, hardware keys). These typically beat Google OAuth on UX since there's no third-party redirect.

To open this to strangers: switch the project's visibility to app tier from the Hatchable console, and we'll add the email + passkey login flow then. For now, you and any invited Hatchable users each get isolated collections.
